\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report digests a working folder that has grown around one claim: that $\C^6$ admits no four mutually
unbiased orthonormal bases, so that $N(6)=3$, as argued in a $76$-page preprint of 24 September 2026. The
folder contains no proof of that claim. It contains something narrower and more useful: fifteen corrections
to the source's statements and to earlier readings of its evidence, six mathematical notes, eleven
computations, and a set of certificate objects whose checks are recorded. The report separates these by
what actually backs them --- a written proof, a named program with its artefact, an outward re-check of a
necessary condition at a stated population, or nothing but an assumption --- because the distance between
those categories is the substance of the work. It records the load-bearing revision (the source's
Theorem~7.1 silently assumes a driver that its formal statement omits), the two interface defects an
independent decoder caught and the repairs that fixed them (a composition block addressing $149$ of $163$
wrong parents; a graph dump valid under eight permutations rather than one), and the headline counts that
do not survive their own tables. It then states the boundary without softening: one Stage-B parent closed
of $163$, four Stage-C records certified of $23{,}506$, $515{,}243$ cells closed by counter rather than by
certificate, $23$ declared Lean holes, and no human gate. The value on offer is not a new theorem but a
redistribution of trust: decisions that once required believing a single binary64 execution are now small
arithmetic facts with artefacts, and the residue is stated as an obligation rather than a result.
\end{abstract}

\section{The folder in one page}

The material divides into six layers, which are best read in this order.



Two conventions matter for reading anything in the folder. First, \emph{radii are measured in cycles}
on the torus $\R^{36}/\Z^{36}$ unless radians are named; the seed radius $0.032$ cycles is $0.201$ rad,
while the Stage-B refinement target $r\le0.03$ is a Euclidean tangent-ball radius. Conflating the two
is the most common error in the notes. Second, \emph{unresolved is never negative}: a cap hit, an
undecided interval, a missing witness or an incomplete cover is an obligation, not an exclusion. The
whole certificate architecture is built to keep that distinction visible.

\paragraph{The load-bearing revision (R1).} The source presents one implication; the formalisation
shows two obligations. A run can satisfy every local inequality it evaluates yet fail to evaluate some
region, and no local certificate covers that region. The Lean statement makes the missing premise a
binder, and the verification paper states it as Theorem~7.1's second half. This is the only revision in
the table that changes what a reader is entitled to conclude from the source's Theorem~7.1 as printed.

\paragraph{The repair that mattered most in practice (R11).} The composition block is the only object
in the run that records \emph{which} unresolved parent each fallback child serves. With the wrong index
space it certified nothing about the capped records while looking well formed: 163 counters, 1,127
children, 11,838 incidences, all internally consistent, all addressing the wrong rows. The repair is
identifier-only --- counters, children and incidence order are unchanged --- and the patch record pins
both emissions (\texttt{699bbc40\ldots} $\to$ \texttt{fd61d257\ldots}), so the difference is itself
auditable. The lesson generalises: a hash-stable object can be semantically wrong, and only an
independent decoder finds it.

\paragraph{Withdrawn claims.} Four claims were asserted inside this programme and retracted; they are
listed so that they are not reintroduced. (i) ``The modular TU split costs $1.9\times$'' --- an artefact
of CPU contention (thirteen agents on four CPUs), retracted by its author; no timing taken while
swarms were live is usable. (ii) ``Lemma~5.1 is false'' --- it is true, with two independent proofs
(the Lean file and a direct derivation from $\norm{x-Tz}^2$). (iii) ``The \texttt{g0} permutation is
unique'' --- eight permutations. (iv) ``The exclusion margin is $1.968\times10^{-9}$'' --- that figure
was taken over the initially open boxes only; the transport margin over the chosen witnesses is
$1.0514\times10^{-9}$.

\section{Proof extensions}

Six pieces of new mathematics sit around the source. They divide sharply into proved statements and
measured ones, and none of them proves $N(6)=3$.

\subsection{The order-three stratum}
\file{results/\allowbreak{}order3-\allowbreak{}stratum.md}, \file{results/\allowbreak{}order3-\allowbreak{}twist.md}

Every MUB triple in $\C^6$ is order-three symmetric (this is the content of the topological
classification, arXiv:2611.06917). The note asks what that symmetry forces on a \emph{fourth} basis.
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Lemma A} (proved): an order-3 symmetry of a flat basis in $\C^6$ has eigenvalue
multiplicities $(2,2,2)$ and acts in two 3-cycles.
\item \textbf{Theorem B} (proved): two order-3 symmetric flat bases are unbiased iff twelve explicit
real algebraic equations of Parseval type hold; verified to machine precision.
\item \textbf{Corollary C} (proved): inside the stratum the ``modulus blindness'' that defeats every
global relaxation disappears --- unbiasedness becomes an equality case.
\item \textbf{Measured}: the stratum contains no 4-tuple; the sharp gap is $\Delta=0.0422687212\dots$
over 500 restarts, attained at a three-level overlap pattern.
\item \textbf{Conjecture}: the gap is exact and certifiable by elimination over $\mathbb{Q}(\omega)$.
\end{itemize}
The second note pushes the reduction further. A \emph{twist lemma} shows that two bases whose frames
differ by scalar twists are unbiased only if the relative twist has vanishing cyclic correlation
(a chirp or constant twist cannot work) --- which is exactly the Fourier triple's behaviour. In the
twisted class (a codimension-3 sub-family with a common flat frame) the pair condition becomes three
explicit trigonometric equations (A),(B1),(B2), and a quartet becomes \emph{ten real variables and
fifteen equations} with no group action and no gauge left. Numerically: 280 isolated twisted pairs
exist; 400 random starts on the three-pair system give a residual floor of $0.386$ (and $0.1096$ in
the overlap form), i.e.\ no quartet, with a wide margin. \emph{Not proved}: the infeasibility. Two
explicit proof shapes are named in \file{order3-\allowbreak{}twist.md} \S4; the common-frame hypothesis is a real
gap, and the general stratum needs one more parameter per basis.

\subsection{Approximate MUBs}
\file{results/\allowbreak{}approx-\allowbreak{}mub6.md} (9 pp.), \file{approx-\allowbreak{}mub6.tex}, \file{work/\allowbreak{}11-\allowbreak{}approx-\allowbreak{}mub/\allowbreak{}}

The source proves $\varepsilon_6^*>0$ and says nothing about its size, where
$\varepsilon_6^*$ is the least achievable maximum deviation of the $36$ pairwise overlaps from $1/6$
over four orthonormal bases. The report brackets it:
\[
0<\varepsilon_6^*\le 0.030224240773 .
\]
The upper bound is \emph{certified} in exact rational arithmetic (a Cayley certificate plus an
explicit perturbation lemma); the best numerical value is $0.0302242407724$, so the certified bound is
within $10^{-12}$ of the search. Four structural results accompany it: the product class
$A\otimes B$ has optimum \emph{exactly} $1/18$ (five independent derivations, and it is far from
optimal); every pair of \emph{real} bases has deviation $\ge1/18$; $\mu_6$-Butson bases obey the same
bound with $1/12$ sharp in the $F_6$ orbit; and no inequality that is a function of the normalised
Gram matrix alone can give a positive lower bound --- it is saturated in $d=6$ by $d=4$ extremal data.
The cyclic slice gives $0.053981857147$ certified. An independent suite, \file{verify/\allowbreak{}verify\_all.py},
re-derives the computational claims from scratch (it imports nothing from the report or from the
member directories).

\subsection{Stronger exclusion inequalities}
\file{results/\allowbreak{}exclusion/\allowbreak{}report.md}, \file{work/\allowbreak{}05-\allowbreak{}parameters/\allowbreak{}}, \file{src/\allowbreak{}exclusion/\allowbreak{}}

This note treats the source's inequality (3.29) as a standalone object on cells rebuilt from the
paper's own definitions (the authors' 249,878-centre dataset is in a different encoding and was not
decoded).
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Theorem A2/A3}: the direction functional is the support function of an explicit compact
convex \emph{cell body} $K$; the optimal margin with Euclidean normalisation is $\mathrm{dist}_2(c,K)$,
attained at the unique $y^*=c-\mathrm{Proj}_K(c)$, with unnormalised margin $\norm{y^*}^2$. The set of
excluding directions is an open convex cone, i.e.\ a second-order-cone programme.
\item \textbf{Theorem B1}: replacing the source's separate error terms by the reverse-triangle
information $\abs{Z_{ij}}\ge\abs{\abs{D_{ij}}-1}$ lowers the right-hand side for every direction with
positive flat weight. Measured: median margin gain $+13.8\%$ (best $+50.7\%$) over $32$ cells --- but
the gain vanishes exactly at cells nearest a completion, so it does not move the exclusion boundary.
\item \textbf{Theorem C1}: a second-order certificate cannot pay for itself: the linearisation
remainder caps the gain at $\tfrac12Y_{\max}E_r^2=6\times10^{-3}$ to $2.4\times10^{-2}$, while the
measured curvature gain is $3\times10^{-3}$.
\item \textbf{Refinement floor}: the right-hand side of (3.29) does not go to zero as $h\to0$; on the
completion's own cell the margin converges to about $-0.48$ and the cell is excluded at no level. That
is what the phase-ball coverage of \S4 is for.
\item \textbf{Calibration}: exact optimisation beats a faithful re-implementation of the paper's
proposer style by $9.3$--$17.7\%$ median margin. All $32$ benchmark cells are excluded by both
methods, so the table measures margin strength, not reach.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The symmetric obstruction, audited}
\file{results/\allowbreak{}symmetric-\allowbreak{}obstruction-\allowbreak{}audit.md}, \file{results/\allowbreak{}verify-\allowbreak{}symmetric-\allowbreak{}obstruction.py}

An external note proposed a threefold-symmetry obstruction. The verdict: the conditional theorem is
correct, short and machine-free, with sharp constants ($1/\sqrt2$ in the lemma, not improvable; a
genuine $1/108$ gap in its \S8, attained), and an explicit realisation of its hypothesis. But it is
\emph{not} a gap-filler: the same conclusion is already in the literature (Sun, arXiv:2610.12180, for
exactly this invariant-ray hypothesis; and Jaming--Matolcsi--M\'ora--Sz\"oll\H{o}si--Weiner,
arXiv:0902.0882, for the whole two-parameter family), and the technique does not generalise --- the
$3\times3$ sign lemma is \emph{false} at four blocks, so $1/\sqrt2$ is $d=6$ numerology. It covers only
MUB-triple slices that were already known, and advances no open step.

\subsection{The largest partial fourth basis}
\file{mub-\allowbreak{}triple-\allowbreak{}maximal.tex} (2 pp.), \file{results/\allowbreak{}}

For a fixed triple, call a unit vector \emph{admissible} if it is unbiased to all three bases, and let
$k$ be the largest number of pairwise orthogonal admissible vectors. The claim is $k=0$ --- strong
unextendibility, far stronger than ``no fourth basis'' --- for every triple that has been decided.
The mechanism is a count: after dephasing, the ten vectors of the last two groups carry $45$ free
phases against $45$ unbiasedness conditions, so the triple is critical; adjoining one vector adds $5$
phases but $10$ conditions, so $\{5,5,5,1\}_6$ is overdetermined by five, and every further vector
costs five more. For the standard $+$ Fourier pair there are exactly $48$ rays unbiased to both, with
$300$ unbiased pairs and $16$ orthonormal sextuples, none of which any ray completes (Grassl's theorem,
reproduced). Product triples and Weyl triples are theorems; the passage to \emph{all} triples is
quoted from the classification and the Sun note, not verified in this folder. The contrast that keeps
the statement honest is $d=4$: the $Z_4$-Weyl triple has $k=0$ (defect $3/16$ exactly) while a triple
drawn from the five MUBs of $\C^4$ has $k=4$. So $N(6)=3$ does not imply $k=0$, and the stronger claim
needs its own argument.

\subsection{Is there an elegant proof?}
\file{results/\allowbreak{}elegant-\allowbreak{}proof-\allowbreak{}routes.md}, \file{mub-\allowbreak{}why-\allowbreak{}no-\allowbreak{}elegant-\allowbreak{}proof.tex} (2 pp.)

Seven routes were examined; none closes. Three abstractions are proved unable to finish the job, and
the most attractive one is dead: \emph{monodromy} (route R7, local constancy of the completion count
off a discriminant) cannot work, because if the singular set has empty interior and is not everything,
the complement is open with completion count $0$ --- there is no proper closed discriminant to
continue around. What survives is a sharply reduced target: extendability forces $E_{23}=\omega$,
$E_{33}=\omega^2$ in the dephased corner, i.e.\ the extendible set lies in the orbit of a $2$-torus
$T_0$; plus the exhaustiveness of the $48$ CAZAC lines in the Fourier chart. That is a finite
classification, not a conceptual proof, and the note says so.

\subsection{The Stage-C replay and what a cap means}
The recorded run is fully reproducible here: $92$ commands, $23{,}506$ records, $2{,}932{,}018{,}956$
completed-negative steps, and the $163$ capped records at exactly $5{,}000{,}001$ steps each
($815{,}000{,}163$ in total). Two facts about the counters are worth more than the totals.
\emph{First}, \file{graph} counts \texttt{stats.positive}, not searches performed: ``graph 0'' does not
say graphs were absent, it says no completed search ended ``pattern possible''. \emph{Second},
Proposition~6.9 has exactly two negative paths, an empty completed bin layer or a completed negative
graph search; a capped search is unresolved and flows to the fallback chain. So the exclusions a
certificate must justify never include a capped search --- and the $163$ capped records' exclusions come
from the fallback pass, which is why the composition block matters (R11) and why raising the cap is not
a free repair.

$1{,}925{,}134{,}199$ steps, max $39{,}968{,}282$ (headroom $31{,}718$ at $4\times10^7$), with zero
candidates found. It costs $39.3$~s of extra search, removes the $126.2$~s fallback pass, nets
$-91.8$~s, shrinks the certificate by $14{,}056{,}421$ bytes and $964$ records, and deletes five
invocations and five Table-1 rows. The measured constant is $\tau=\max(0,R-X_M)$ with
$s\le R+1.001\times10^{-9}$.

\section{Formalisation}

\file{work/\allowbreak{}04-\allowbreak{}lean/\allowbreak{}Mub6/\allowbreak{}} holds $19$ Lean~4 modules against Mathlib. The audited count (gate G4) is
$23$ declared holes over $7$ files, each named in the status tables; a current grep finds $27$
textual occurrences of \texttt{sorry} in $8$ of the $19$ modules. The two counts are different measurements --- declared
obligations against lines of text, some in commentary --- and neither is a proof of absence.
The three verification modules \file{StageCNecessity.lean}, \file{StageCGraph.lean} and
\file{AssemblyScoped.lean} contain none, and their chain lemmas depend only on the standard axioms.
Eleven of the $19$ modules contain no \texttt{sorry} at all; among them \file{Completion.lean},
\file{Refine.lean} and \file{Assembly.lean} are claim-free stubs that assert nothing.

What the formalisation establishes is an \emph{assembly interface}: if the seed cover is valid, the
Stage-A tree and Stage-B ledger cover the regions up to the prescribed equivalences, the Stage-C
records are terminal negative, and the driver's terminal conditions hold, then no point of the initial
set is extendible by two further bases. Both finite inductions (over subdivision depth and over
completed parent records) are formal. What it does not establish is any instance of those hypotheses in
the recorded run --- and, per R1, the source's Theorem~7.1 as printed does not even state the driver
obligation that the formal version binds.

Three further limitations are recorded rather than repaired: \S5.1--5.6 (the Stage-B relations) are
not stated at all; Lemma~6.1's \texttt{bin\_displacement} is the only Stage-C hole; and the paper's
Lemma~2.2 transpose clause is invalid as printed. The companion preprint \file{work/\allowbreak{}08-\allowbreak{}paper/\allowbreak{}mub6-\allowbreak{}formal.pdf}
(11 pp.) reports exactly this scope, with the $23$ holes listed rather than summarised. The sentence a
reader must not take away from it is ``the main result was formalised'': the development contains
conditional implications, and the arithmetic and execution premises are binders, not theorems.

\section{The boundary}

What the folder does not contain, stated once and plainly:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=1.8em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{No proof of $N(6)=3$ produced here.} The source's theorem is conditional on an execution
and on seven programs implementing the specification; the folder verifies local fragments of that
evidence, not the theorem.
\item \textbf{No complete instantiation of the certificate.} One parent out of $163$ has a closed
Stage-B ledger; four Stage-C records out of $23{,}506$ have certified predicates; one record has a
local graph exclusion; $515{,}243$ inside cells are counter-only.
\item \textbf{No human gate.} No person has approved any result. The decision log's H0 entries authorise a
one-hour feasibility phase, not the programme --- and its identifiers collide (D6--D10 each appear
twice), so cite it by title, not by number. The six certificate gates H0--H5 are at most ``ready for a
decision''.
\item \textbf{No peer review.} Nothing here has been refereed. The independent proof of the same
theorem (Wuttig--Tindall, arXiv:2611.06917, 4 November 2026) is cited but was not verified in-session,
and one extension (the $k=0$ claim for all triples) leans on it.
\item \textbf{No authenticated run.} The source's $4$~October execution is attested by its archive;
the $7$~September run is described by the paper itself as unauthenticated; the record's provenance is
not independently established here.
\item \textbf{No portable arithmetic.} The source's binary64-plus-margins model, its
\texttt{-march=native} build and its two \texttt{pow} sites are load-bearing for one documented run.
The folder's own checks are exact or outward where they matter, but they do not cover the whole run.
\end{enumerate}

Conversely, what the folder does contain that a reader can use without believing any of the above:
a byte-reproducible covering stage; a $5.7$~GiB witness stream with a five-term ledger and zero
missing pairs; a repaired and independently decoded composition block; a closed local Stage-B cover
with exact composition algebra; twenty exact directional certificates with $360$ controls; a
certificate checker for four Stage-C rules with no undecided intervals; $34$ explicit Root witnesses;

\section{Open items, in the order the evidence suggests}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{O\arabic*.},leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Ball and cluster coverage for Stage C.} The predicates are about balls; the missing
premise is that every admissible bin lies in one of them. This is the single prerequisite for turning
Propositions~5.2--5.3 into a local exclusion, and it should be done outward on the same four records
before any graph claim.
\item \textbf{One fully certified Stage-A seed.} Twenty directional certificates exist; the analytic
implication (3.26) and a complete retained tree do not. The right target is one tiny seed, complete,
not a production run.
\item \textbf{A real Stage-B chain.} No parent$\to$child$\to$Stage-C instance exists among $1{,}896$
candidates. The acceptable next object is one parent whose own ledger, transformed child ledger and
terminal Stage-C record are all present and checked.
\item \textbf{The nine Root witnesses.} Either explicit $M$-witnesses at the same standard as the
$34$, or a rigorous obstruction. A failed search is not an obstruction.
\item \textbf{Predicates at scale.} Run the basic and joint checkers over all completed-negative and
fallback records before assembling graph evidence; keep the two vacuous records distinguished.
\item \textbf{Instantiate the Lean assembly.} Connect every \texttt{Cov} and terminal premise to a
decoded certificate; until then the formal and numerical branches do not meet.
\item \textbf{Finish the code-recreation gates.} G-R1 passed (byte identity); G-R2--G-R5 pending
(\file{coarse*.bin}, \file{mid.bin}, \file{fine.bin}, the $100$-command replay, handover).
\item \textbf{The analytic extension programme.} The order-three twist system (ten variables, fifteen
equations) has a numerical floor and no proof; the common-frame hypothesis of \file{order3-\allowbreak{}twist.md}
\S2 is a real gap; the $k=0$ claim for all triples awaits an independent check of arXiv:2611.06917.
\item \textbf{A human review and a decision.} No gate is approved. The next move that is not
mechanical is a person reading the arithmetic contracts, running the checkers on fresh copies, and
deciding whether the trusted base is acceptable.
\end{enumerate}
