## 1. The result

`spine.md` §3 proves: **the paper's main theorem is equivalent to**

> seed cover (Prop. 2.10) + completion coverage (Prop. 4.16) + two refinement passes
> (Prop. 5.7 / Cor. 5.8) + local exclusion (Prop. 6.9) + seven bookkeeping predicates X1–X7 of §7.2,

with nothing else used and no step left implicit. The proof is a chain of four cover transfers
between regions of one space plus two applications of Stage C, and it is machine-checked in
`lean/Interface.lean`: `assembly`, `assembly_fallback`, `refines_trans`, `excludes_of_refines`
all elaborate with `lean` and `#print axioms` reports "does not depend on any axioms".

The statement simplification that makes this work: every stage conclusion in §§2–6 has the same
shape, `Rep(|in|) ⊆ Rep(|out|)` for the equivalence relation generated by Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 4.7,
so the assembly is transitivity of one relation rather than six bespoke arguments. This is a
reformulation for T8/T4, not a change of content.

## 2. What this direction adds beyond the catalogue

**(a) The assembly, with quantifiers and in machine-checkable form.** `results/proof-claims.md` §1
gives the six-link skeleton as a diagram; `spine.md` §2–§3 gives each link with its hypotheses
bound, and the composition as a theorem. `lean/Interface.lean` fixes the interface: three
primitives (`mem` = class membership, `Exact`, `Ext`) and six hypotheses. Five of the six are the
paper's statements verbatim; the sixth (`hC`) needs one lemma (`spine.md` Lemma S.2) that the paper
does not state, and a formaliser who misses it gets a non-composing statement. That is the single
most useful thing this file gives T4.

**(b) A repair to the proof of Lemma 2.2 (finding F2).** Lemma 2.2's proof says *"A row
permutation or row rephasing is a common unitary operation on all four bases; it preserves the
standard basis up to the same irrelevant changes."* The conclusion is true, the sentence is not:
applying a diagonal unitary `D` to the four bases gives `(D, DH/√6, DB3, DB4)`, whose first member
is not the coordinate basis, so extendibility of `DH` in the sense of Definition 2.1 does not
follow. Repair (`spine.md` Lemma S.6): monomial unitaries preserve entrywise moduli, so
`(I, VM, VB3, VB4)` are four MUBs whenever `(I, M, B3, B4)` are. Two lines, conclusion unchanged.
`results/proof-claims.md` says of Lemma 2.2 that "transposition is the only non-obvious case"; the
row-rephasing case is the other one, and its printed proof does not run.

**(c) Prop. 4.16 is weaker in its statement than in its proof (finding F3).** It covers "every
exact second-basis Hadamard matrix *required for the four-basis problem*", while its proof follows
*any* feasible exact matrix through the cell tree. The qualifier can be deleted; the assembly uses
the stronger form and the stronger form is what is proved.

**(d) An answer to the catalogue's open question on the ranges of (6.2) (finding F4).**
`claims.yaml` (`prop-6.9`) calls the admitted ranges `n0<1e-6, ι<1e-4, Γ<20, ω<1e-5,
mnorm<2, froot<1e-6` "the sharpest internal gap", on the ground that Definition 4.1 does not
contain them and §§4–5 are not shown to produce them. They are produced, twice:
§5.6, p. 39 — *"the normal constants are nonnegative and are checked against γP<20, n0P, εP<1e-6,
αP<1e-4, oP, horP<1e-5, µP<2"* — and §A.9, p. 67 — *"`validate` enforces 0≤R≤.126, 0≤C<20,
0≤n0,froot<1e-6, 0≤id<1e-4, 0≤ort,hor<1e-5, 0≤mnorm<2, 0≤k≤10, |p_j|≤4"*. So a Record whose
constants violate a range is not admitted, which fails its seed or parent, which is exactly what
terminal conditions X2/X3/X4 exclude. This is weaker than "proved" and stronger than "not known":
the ranges hold for every Record that reached Stage C **conditional on the run's terminal
conditions**. T9 should still check that a validation abort is distinguishable from a failed seed
in the evidence, because that distinguishability is what carries the argument.

**(e) What the assembly does not need (finding F6).** The dual support bound (2.19), the ellipsoid
machinery of §4.5 and the clique search of Lemma 6.8 are each consumed inside one stage and never
by the assembly. Consequence for T2/T3: a regression that breaks them damages the *efficiency* of
a search, not the validity of the chain, provided the cover still completes; the reference model
may legally use the brute-force midpoint radius `3/(2N)` in place of the reuse test.

## 3. Corroborations (independently reached, and already in `T1-report.md`)

* **Radius composability (their I4, my F1).** Stage C needs `r ≤ .03`; only the `.016` (fine) and
  `.011` (fallback) passes qualify, never `.07` (mid) nor `.125` (A). `spine.md` §4 records it as
  interface I5 and adds the general point: the constraint is *arithmetic* (it enters only through
  the Appendix A margins and range checks), so if T5/T2 re-certify at a larger radius the interface
  widens.
* **The §2/§3 coordinate correspondence (their I7, my I2).** Supplied by the paper at the end of
  §2.4 and by (3.2); the assembly only has to cite it.
* **Units (their I8, my §6).** `spine.md` §6 states the same ledger as a table with the three
  bridges `s=2π(q-u)`, `R_s=2πr_s`, `δ=Δ+Π(ε+u/2π)`, and marks the seed bridge as
  *completeness*-critical rather than soundness-critical: taking `R_s` in cycles makes the seed
  ball 6.3× too small, so the cover would silently fail to be exhaustive.
* **The bracket of (5.1) (their notation question, my I5/I7).** `spine.md` §4.1 gives the two
  readings and shows that only the generative one composes.

## 6. Places where I believe the paper is internally inconsistent

Marked **[new]** where `T1-report.md` does not already list them.

* **[new] F2.** Lemma 2.2's proof, row rephasing: the printed sentence does not establish the
  stated conclusion (repair in `spine.md` §5.3). Severity: proof, not statement.
* **[new] F3.** Prop. 4.16's "required for the four-basis problem" is weaker than its own proof.
  Severity: cosmetic, but it makes the assembly look as if it needed an extendibility hypothesis
  it does not need.
* **[new] F4.** Matrix of ranges: Def. 4.1 has no ranges, §5.6 and §A.9 do; §6.1 repeats four of
  them. Three statements of one condition, none of them in the definition where a reader looks.
  Severity: comprehension; the condition is met.
* **F1 = their I4.** Stage C's `r ≤ .03` versus Stage B's `τ ≤ .126`.
* **Their I1** ((6.8)'s conjugation) is not used by the assembly at all — it is inside Lemma 6.3 —
  but it is the most consequential statement error they found, and I agree with the disposition.
* **A structural observation for T8.** Theorem 1.1 is *literally* "Theorem 7.1 plus the observed
  run"; there is no step of mathematics between them. The paper is honest about this in §7 and §8
  but the abstract reads as if the theorem were unconditional modulo "arithmetic and compiler
  conditions". Recommend the abstract name the execution record, e.g. "subject to the checks of
  §7.2 on the recorded execution of §7.5".
