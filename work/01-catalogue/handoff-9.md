## The one-sentence result

The paper's main theorem is *equivalent* to: seed cover (Prop. 2.10) + completion coverage
(Prop. 4.16) + two refinement passes (Prop. 5.7 / Cor. 5.8) + local exclusion (Prop. 6.9) +
seven bookkeeping predicates X1–X7 of §7.2. Nothing else in the paper is used by the top-level
argument and no step of the composition is implicit. Proved in `spine.md` §3, machine-checked in
`lean/Interface.lean`.

## For T4

Read `spine.md` §1 and §3, then `lean/Interface.lean`. Three primitives:

| Lean | paper |
|---|---|
| `mem : Reg → P → Prop` | "the equivalence class of `H` meets the region `R`" — **class** membership, absorbing every "up to equivalence" clause at once |
| `Exact : P → Prop` | `F(H) = 0`, i.e. `e(H)` complex Hadamard (`spine.md` Lemma S.1) |
| `Ext : P → Prop` | extendible, with the coordinate basis, by two further MUBs |

and six hypotheses `h0, hA, hBm, hBf, hBat, hC`. Five are the paper's stage statements verbatim.
**`hC` is the only one that needs a lemma of `spine.md` (Lemma S.2)**, because Prop. 6.9 speaks
about the ball while the assembly needs the class; transcribing `hC` without Lemma S.2 gives a
statement that does not compose. Lemma S.2 is proved in `spine.md` §5.3, including the
transposition case and the monomial repair of finding F2.

Two traps:

1. Do **not** model `mem` as plain set membership. `Rep` twice must be `Rep` (Corollary S.3(a));
   without that the two B passes do not compose.
2. Carry Stage C's radius side condition, `r ≤ .03` (finding F1).

## For T8

Four edits, in order of value.

1. **Add one joint equivalence lemma** in §2 (`spine.md` Lemma S.2): extendibility is invariant
   under permutations, rephasings, integer shifts, conjugation, transposition and global sign.
   The paper proves Lemma 2.2 (partly) and Lemma 4.7 (partly) and never puts them together, yet
   every "up to equivalence" clause in §§2–6 uses the union.
2. **Fix one sentence in the proof of Lemma 2.2.** "A row permutation or row rephasing is a
   common unitary operation on all four bases; it preserves the standard basis up to the same
   irrelevant changes" — applying a diagonal unitary to the coordinate basis gives a different
   basis, so it does not establish extendibility as Definition 2.1 defines it. The conclusion is
   true; the repair is the two-line monomial transport of `spine.md` Lemma S.6.
3. **State the radius ordering.** Prop. 6.9 needs `r ≤ .03`; A emits `.125`, B mid `.07`; only
   B fine's `.016` (and the fallback's `.011`) qualify. One sentence where §6 begins.
4. **Replace the vague bracket of (5.1).** "[…] the equivalence classes used in the preceding
   sections, including the permitted signed permutation and transpose operations" reads as a
   closed list but must be read as "generated by" (`spine.md` §4.1, I7). Define the class once.

Also useful: `spine.md` §6 is a units table for the hand-offs, §7 is twelve overloaded words with
